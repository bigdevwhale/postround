# Privacy — Poste Restante («До востребования»)

The game keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics.

- **Location.** Used to know which house you stand at, so mail can be handed over, and to draw your district. It is stored only on the phone. The district map is downloaded from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data) for a square around your home; the weather comes from MET Norway for your rounded coordinates. Nothing else leaves the phone.
- **Steps (Health Connect).** Only if you turn it on in Settings. The game reads your daily step totals to size the morning bag and show how far you walked. Steps are never written back, sent anywhere or shared.
- **Notifications.** Used for the round in progress, telegrams and mail handed over on the way.

You can disconnect Health Connect in Settings at any time; uninstalling the app deletes all its data.
